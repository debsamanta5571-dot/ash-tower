# Ash Tower

A deckbuilding roguelike.

![Ash Tower](Docs/preview.gif)

Someone once told me you aren't a real game developer until you've made a roguelike deckbuilder. Tell me if you can guess the inspiration.

You are the Cinder Knight. Climb the tower, collect cards and relics, and fight your way to the final boss.

## Contents

- 54 cards
- 9 enemies
- 29 relics
- 10 potions
- 6 events

## Mechanics

**Deckbuilding**

You climb with a deck that actually changes. Cards move through a draw pile, a hand, and a discard pile. Attacks, skills, and powers cost energy. Some exhaust out of the fight. Camps let you upgrade, shops and rewards let you add, and a few events will stick something ugly in there on purpose.

**Energy**

You get a fresh pool of energy at the start of every turn. Spend it, generate more mid-fight, or hold a little back. Relics can turn leftover energy into block, discount the first card of a fight, or pay you for shuffling.

**Relics**

Relics sit in the background and rewrite the rules. They can fire when a fight starts, when you play a card, when you take a hit, or when the draw pile runs out. You find them in shops, events, and elite rewards, and they stack.

**Combat**

Enemies telegraph what they are about to do. Block soaks the hit. Statuses are not copies of someone else's game: Heft burns at the end of your turn, Brittle punches through block, Dulled cuts damage, Unsteady punishes standing open, and Nails bite back when their block gets chipped.

## Play

[Download for Windows](https://github.com/debsamanta5571-dot/ash-tower/releases/download/v0.1/AshTower-Windows.zip)

Unzip and run `AshTower.exe`.

## Features

- Card combat with intent, block, status, and relic hooks
- Procedural climb with a map
- Cards, relics, potions, and events
- Shop, rest, and reward screens

Made with Unity 6.3 LTS (`6000.3.0f1`).
